## Name

ImageCrate

## Short description (132 characters or fewer)

Choose images on the current page and save them together.

## Detailed description

ImageCrate shows the images on the page you are viewing, lets you choose the ones you want, and saves that selection on your device.

It does not crawl other pages, insert ads, or record the screen. The workspace is shown in Japanese.

What you can do:

- List the images already on the open page.
- Keep ad-like images out of the list at first, and bring them back when you want them.
- Look at an image more closely and select only what you need.
- Save the selection to Chrome's download folder, a folder you choose, or one ZIP file.
- Convert to JPEG, PNG, or WebP on your device.

Installing ImageCrate does not grant access to every site. Images on the same site as the page you opened can be previewed with the temporary access from the toolbar click. An image hosted somewhere else is requested only when you press that host's button. Saving the original file to Chrome's download folder does not ask for more site access. Saving to a folder, making a ZIP, or converting asks only for the hosts of the images you selected.

Processing stays on your device. Images and page addresses are not sent to the developer. See the privacy policy for the details.

Save only images you have the right to keep.
